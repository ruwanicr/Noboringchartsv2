How to read the timeline: The four bars are DCS mid-year estimates. The 2024 census is a separate count at 19 December, not another July estimate. The 2025 mid-year estimate is provisional and uses births, deaths and net migration after the census moment. Estimates can be revised; the chart keeps the source's rounded thousand-person precision. The final census count is 18,630 above the preliminary release. In the ethnic-groups view, tiny groups are listed precisely, but their short bars are below the chart's easy-to-read scale.

Sources & notes

Annual estimates: Department of Census and Statistics, Statistical Data Sheet 2025 (2022–24, in thousands); Registrar General's Department, Mid-year population review, October 2025 (provisional 1 July 2025).

Final count and breakdowns: DCS, Census of Population and Housing 2024 Final Report, census moment 19 December 2024.

Tables: DCS Basic Population Tables (sector table A1; ethnic group table A3); CPH-2024 portal (district, sex, age, ethnicity, religion, sector, GN and DS-level data).

Cross-check: World Bank WDI API, SP.POP.TOTL (2022: 22.181m; 2023: 22.037m; 2024: 21.916m; 2025: 21.756m). This series aligns with mid-year estimates, not the census count.

Population changes refer to estimates; the census breakdowns are a one-time snapshot. Values are not interchangeable or directly comparable across reference dates.
